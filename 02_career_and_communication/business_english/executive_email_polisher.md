# ✉️ Executive Email & Communication Polisher

Use this prompt to elevate your day-to-day business communication—transforming rough thoughts, quick notes, or bullet points into confident, polished, and culturally fluent business English.

---

## 🎭 Tone Guide

| Style | When to Use | Key Trait |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive / Concise** | Senior leaders, directors, VPs | Front-loads the "Bottom Line Up Front" (BLUF); short sentences; bulleted asks. |
| **Assertive & Clear** | Blockers, scope changes, boundary setting | Direct, polite, non-apologetic, clear owners and deadlines. |
| **Diplomatic & Collaborative** | Cross-team negotiations, pushback | Focuses on shared objectives, empathy, and alternative solutions. |
| **Warm & Inspiring** | Team updates, sprint celebration, retros | High energy, highlights collective contributions, encourages ownership. |

---

## 🔄 Quick Phrasing Upgrades (Say This, Not That)

| Instead of (Passive / Apologetic) | Use This (Professional & Confident) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Sorry to bother you..."* | *"Thank you for your time on this."* |
| *"I think maybe we should consider..."* | *"I recommend we proceed with..."* |
| *"Hopefully that makes sense?"* | *"Please let me know if you would like to adjust any details."* |
| *"We are late because team X didn't respond."* | *"We have an external dependency on team X that is impacting our target delivery date."* |

## 🤖 The AI Prompt (Copy & Paste into Chat)

```markdown
You are an executive communications coach and native business English specialist.

Help me refine my draft message into polished, professional business English.

### My Inputs:
- **Target Audience:** [e.g., Executive Sponsor, Engineering Manager, Development Team, Client]
- **Desired Tone:** [Executive & Concise / Assertive & Direct / Diplomatic / Warm]
- **Core Message / Rough Notes:**
[PASTE YOUR DRAFT EMAIL OR ROUGH BULLET POINTS HERE]

---

### Please provide:
1. **Polished Version:** The final draft ready to send, including a crisp Subject Line and clear Call to Action (CTA).
2. **Key Changes & Rationale:** 2–3 brief bullet points explaining why specific words or sentence structures were changed to make the communication more effective.
3. **Alternative Subject Lines:** 2 other compelling subject line options.
```
