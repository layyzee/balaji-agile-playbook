# ⛵ Sailboat Retrospective Template

The **Sailboat Retrospective** is a visual and engaging format that helps teams look forward to goals while identifying what is driving them forward or holding them back.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Agenda (45–60 Mins)

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Icebreaker & Context** | 5 mins | Welcome the team, state the sprint goal and purpose of the retro. |
| **2. Silent Brainstorming** | 10 mins | Team members add sticky notes to the 4 quadrants without talking. |
| **3. Group & Review** | 15 mins | Facilitator clusters similar themes and reads them aloud. |
| **4. Dot Voting** | 5 mins | Each member gets 3 votes on the topics most critical to address. |
| **5. Action Planning** | 15–20 mins | Define 1–3 concrete, actionable SMART improvements for the next sprint. |

---

## 🗺️ The 4 Sailboat Quadrants

```text
       ⛵ THE BOAT (The Team)
         |
         v
-----------------------------------------------------------------
💨 WIND (What propelled us forward?)
   - Tools, practices, or team wins that helped us move faster.
   - Example: "Pair programming on PR #104 saved 2 days of testing."

⚓ ANCHORS (What held us back or slowed us down?)
   - Delays, waiting times, blockers, or process friction.
   - Example: "Slow CI/CD build times blocked code merges."

🪨 ROCKS / RISKS (What future obstacles could sink our ship?)
   - Upcoming architectural bottlenecks, key-person dependencies, holidays.
   - Example: "Three developers on leave during the upcoming release sprint."

🏝️ THE ISLAND (What is our goal / destination?)
   - What does a successful next sprint or release look like?
   - Example: "Zero open critical bugs by release cut-off."
-----------------------------------------------------------------
```
