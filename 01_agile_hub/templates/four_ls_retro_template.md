# 🍀 4Ls Retrospective Template (Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For)

The **4Ls format** is a balanced retrospective that highlights positive achievements, celebrates continuous learning, and constructively surfaces gaps and aspirations.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Agenda (45–60 Mins)

| Phase | Duration | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Set the Stage** | 5 mins | Read the Agile Prime Directive: *"Regardless of what we discover, we understand and truly believe that everyone did the best job they could..."* |
| **2. Silent Writing** | 10 mins | Team writes cards across the 4 categories. |
| **3. Group Discussion** | 20 mins | Walk through each category together, focusing on patterns and insights. |
| **4. Prioritize & Vote** | 5 mins | Vote on the top 1–2 items that need process intervention. |
| **5. Action Items** | 15 mins | Assign owners and clear completion criteria. |

---
