# Balaji AI Workbench: Comprehensive Guide

This guide explains how each section of your AI Workbench is organized, how you can use it, and how to work alongside your AI assistant to achieve your goals.

---

## 📁 Section Overview

### `01_agile_hub/` — Scrum Master & Agile Coaching
Designed to be your daily Agile copilot.
- **`templates/`**: Ready-to-use markdown templates for Sprint Retrospectives (e.g., Sailboat, 4Ls, Mad/Sad/Glad), Sprint Planning agendas, and Definition of Done checklists.
- **`prompts/`**: Curated prompts for handling challenging team dynamics, facilitating backlog refinement, and reframing user stories with INVEST criteria.
- **`metrics_analyzers/`**: Simple Python scripts or spreadsheet analyzers to calculate team velocity, cycle time, and sprint burndown trends.

### `02_career_and_communication/` — Business English & Interview Skills
Designed to elevate your executive communication and prepare you for high-stakes conversations.
- **`interview_prep/`**: Question banks (behavioral, situational, Agile leadership) and your personal **STAR-method Story Bank** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- **`business_english/`**: Templates and AI prompts to transform informal thoughts into crisp, professional stakeholder updates and executive emails.
- **`recordings_and_notes/`**: A personal repository to track interview practice feedback, pronunciation notes, and vocabulary builders.

### `03_automations/` — Professional Task Automation
Designed to eliminate repetitive manual overhead.
- **`meeting_assistants/`**: Tools and scripts to paste meeting notes/transcripts and automatically extract action items, owners, and decisions.
- **`report_generators/`**: Automated templates to turn Jira or standup updates into executive summary bullets.
- **`scheduled_tasks/`**: Background automation scripts (e.g., sending yourself a morning digest or reminder).

### `04_apps_and_dashboards/` — Interactive Applications
Designed to help you build working visual tools without needing deep web development knowledge.
- **`sprint_dashboard/`**: Beginner-friendly Streamlit or HTML apps to visualize sprint status and metrics with charts.
- **`team_health_tracker/`**: An interactive tool for collecting and displaying team sentiment or retro votes.

### `05_agents_and_skills/` — Reusable AI Agents & Skills
Designed to house custom AI capabilities you can invoke repeatedly.
- **`agents/`**: System prompts and persona definitions (e.g., *Agile Coach*, *Senior Interviewer*, *Executive Editor*).
- **`skills/`**: Standardized Antigravity skills (`SKILL.md`) that teach your AI assistant specific multi-step workflows.

### `06_learning_lab/` — Safe Learning Sandbox
Designed for hands-on, zero-risk learning.
- **`python_basics/`**: Step-by-step beginner exercises (variables, loops, functions, reading CSVs).
- **`api_playground/`**: Clear walkthroughs of how APIs work (calling Gemini, Jira, or weather APIs with simple Python scripts).
- **`mcp_experiments/`**: Exploration of Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and tools.
- **`notes/`**: Quick-reference cheat sheets for Git, terminal commands, and AI concepts.

## 💡 How to Work with Your AI Assistant

As a beginner, you don't need to write code from scratch. You can use this workbench by following this flow:

1. **State your objective clearly:** For example:
   > *"I want to create a Sailboat Retrospective template in `01_agile_hub/templates`."*
   > *"I want to write my first Python script in `06_learning_lab/python_basics` that prints my sprint goals."*
2. **Ask for explanations:** Whenever code or technical jargon appears, ask:
   > *"Explain how this code works line by line like I am a beginner."*
3. **Build iteratively:** Start small, test immediately, and refine based on your real day-to-day work.
